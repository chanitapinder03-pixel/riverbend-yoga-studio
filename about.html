Meet Our Instructors

Maya Collins - Lead Instructor

Maya teaches gentle and vinyasa classes with an emphasis on clear instruction, steady breathing, and practical modifications. She enjoys helping beginners feel comfortable while also giving experienced students room to grow.

Daniel Reed - Restorative Instructor

Daniel focuses on restorative practice and mindful movement. His classes encourage students to slow down, use supportive props, and choose positions that feel sustainable and comfortable.

Accessibility Accommodations

Riverbend welcomes students with different mobility, sensory, and communication needs. The studio can provide chair-based pose options, extra transition time, space near the entrance, and verbal descriptions of movements. Students are encouraged to contact the studio before class if a specific accommodation would make participation easier.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive about 10 minutes before class when possible.
  • Silence mobile devices before practice begins.
  • Tell the instructor about injuries, limitations, or accommodation needs before class.
  • Move within your own comfort level and rest whenever necessary.
  • If you cannot attend a reserved workshop, contact the studio as early as possible.
