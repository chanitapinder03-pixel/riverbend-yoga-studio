Class Types

Gentle Yoga

A slower-paced class with simple movements, longer transitions, and supportive options. This class is a good choice for beginners, older adults, and students returning to movement.

Vinyasa Yoga

A flowing practice that connects movement with breathing. Students can expect a steady sequence of poses with modifications offered for different experience levels.

Restorative Yoga

A quiet practice that uses comfortable, supported poses to encourage rest and relaxation. It is well suited for students who want to unwind after a busy day.

Private Sessions

One-on-one sessions are available for students who want individualized guidance, help learning foundational poses, or a practice adapted to personal mobility and comfort needs. Private sessions are scheduled by request.

Pricing Overview

Riverbend uses a simple pay-per-class and class-pass model. Students may choose a single class, a multi-class pass, or a private-session rate depending on their needs. Payment is handled directly with the studio; no online checkout is required on this website.

  • Single class: one studio class
  • Five-class pass: flexible use across group classes
  • Monthly pass: suitable for regular attendance
  • Private session: individually scheduled instruction
